# Lumora: Skincare Rituals, Application Sequencing, and Skin Profiles

## 1. The Lumora 4-Step Baseline Ritual
We recommend a streamlined four-step ritual for sustained epidermal health:
1. **Cleanse:** Remove impurities without stripping the natural lipid mantle. Use lukewarm water; never hot water, which triggers vascular flushing.
2. **Prep & Tone:** Balance epidermal pH and enhance subsequent active penetration using hydrating, alcohol-free essences.
3. **Target (Serums):** Apply high-efficacy concentrated bio-actives to address specific skin concerns (e.g. barrier restoration, pigmentation, dehydration).
4. **Lock & Protect:** Seal moisture with biomimetic lipid creams. In morning rituals, always finish with broad-spectrum SPF 50+ mineral or hybrid sunscreen.

## 2. Product Layering Principles
- **Thinnest to Thickest:** Apply water-soluble, low-viscosity products first (toners, essences), followed by light serums (hyaluronic acid, niacinamide), emulsion serums (vitamin C, retinoids), creams, and finally facial oils or sunscreens.
- **Active Separation:**
  - *Retinoids & Direct Acids (AHA/BHA):* Do not combine on the same evening to avoid barrier compromise. Alternate evenings.
  - *Vitamin C & Niacinamide:* Lumora's stabilized Vitamin C serum is buffered to be fully compatible with our Niacinamide serum, but sensitive skin types may prefer applying Vitamin C in the morning and Niacinamide in the evening.

## 3. Recommendations by Skin Concern & Climate in Egypt
- **Oily and Blemish-Prone Skin (Humid Summer Weather):**
  - Gentle Salicylic Acid Clarifying Gel Cleanser.
  - 10% Niacinamide + Zinc 1% Oil-Control Serum.
  - Ultra-lightweight Hydro-Water Gel Moisturizer.
- **Dry, Dehydrated, or Barrier-Impaired Skin (Air-Conditioned or Winter Environments):**
  - Ceramide Hydrating Cream Cleanser.
  - 2% Multi-Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Serum.
  - 5-Ceramide Barrier Repair Recovery Cream.
- **Sensitive and Reactive Skin:**
  - Centella Asiatica & Panthenol Soothing Mist.
  - Calming Oat Lipid Barrier Serum.
  - Fragrance-free Mineral Sheer Shield SPF 50+.

## 4. The Golden Rule of Patch Testing
We advise all patrons to perform a 24-hour patch test before introducing any active formulation:
1. Apply a dime-sized drop behind the ear or on the inner forearm.
2. Allow to absorb and leave unwashed for 24 hours.
3. If no redness, itching, burning, or swelling occurs, safely introduce the product into your facial ritual, starting 2 to 3 times weekly.
