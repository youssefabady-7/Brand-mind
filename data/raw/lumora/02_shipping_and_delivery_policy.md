# Lumora: Shipping, Delivery Timelines, and Order Tracking Policy

## 1. Shipping Coverage & Domestic Courier Partners
Lumora ships domestically to all governorates across the Arab Republic of Egypt via trusted local courier logistics (Bosta, Aramex, and specialized temperature-regulated domestic carriers).

## 2. Standard Shipping Times and Delivery Windows
- **Greater Cairo & Giza (including New Cairo, 6th of October, Sheikh Zayed, Maadi, Nasr City, Heliopolis):**
  - Standard Delivery: 1 to 2 business days.
  - Express Same-Day Option: Available for orders confirmed before 12:00 PM (Cairo Time) at an additional premium.
- **Alexandria and Delta Governorates (Tanta, Mansoura, Zagazig, Damanhour):**
  - Standard Delivery: 2 to 3 business days.
- **Canal Cities (Port Said, Ismailia, Suez):**
  - Standard Delivery: 2 to 3 business days.
- **Upper Egypt (Beni Suef, Minya, Asyut, Sohag, Qena, Luxor, Aswan):**
  - Standard Delivery: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Red Sea & Frontier Regions (Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, Marsa Matrouh, North/South Sinai):**
  - Standard Delivery: 4 to 6 business days.

*Note: Business days are Sunday through Thursday. Orders placed on Friday, Saturday, or national public holidays are processed the following business day.*

## 3. Shipping Rates & Free Shipping Thresholds
- **Flat Rate Standard Shipping (Cairo & Giza):** 45 EGP.
- **Flat Rate Standard Shipping (Alexandria & Delta):** 60 EGP.
- **Flat Rate Standard Shipping (Upper Egypt & Red Sea):** 75 EGP.
- **Complimentary Free Shipping:** All domestic orders exceeding **800 EGP** qualify for automatic free standard shipping across all governorates.

## 4. Order Confirmation, Dispatch, and Real-Time Tracking
- Upon placing an order, patrons receive an instant SMS and email confirmation containing a unique synthetic order reference (format: `LUM-XXXX`, e.g., `LUM-1042`).
- When the order is packed and dispatched from our temperature-controlled central fulfillment hub in Cairo, a live tracking link with the courier tracking code is sent via WhatsApp and SMS.
- Couriers make up to 3 delivery attempts before returning the parcel to our hub. Patrons may reschedule delivery windows directly with the courier dispatch team.
