# Lumora: Ingredients Transparency, Pregnancy Safety, and Product FAQs

## 1. Frequently Asked Questions on Formulations

### Q1: Are Lumora products safe for pregnant and nursing mothers?
Most Lumora hydrating, soothing, and barrier-repair formulations (Hyaluronic Acid Serum, Ceramide Cream, Centella Mist, Mineral SPF) are formulated without teratogenic ingredients and are considered safe during pregnancy.
**Important Exception:** Products containing *Retinoids / Retinol* or high concentrations of *Salicylic Acid (BHA > 1%)* should be avoided during pregnancy and lactation. Always consult your personal OB/GYN before starting active skincare treatments.

### Q2: What is the shelf life and Period After Opening (PAO)?
- **Unopened Shelf Life:** All Lumora products have an unopened shelf life of 24 months from the manufacturing batch date printed on the bottom of the carton.
- **Period After Opening (PAO):** Indicated by the open-jar icon on the container:
  - Serums with active antioxidants (Vitamin C, Retinol): 6 months after opening.
  - Hydrating cleansers, toners, and moisturizers: 12 months after opening.
  - Sunscreens: 12 months after opening.

### Q3: How should Lumora products be stored in hot Egyptian summers?
Store products in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight and excessive humidity. Room temperatures below 25°C are ideal. Avoid leaving serums on sunlit window sills or in hot vehicles. Serums like Pure Vitamin C and Soothing Toners may be stored in a skincare refrigerator (8°C to 12°C) for an enhanced de-puffing sensory experience.

### Q4: Are your products non-comedogenic and vegan?
Yes. 100% of Lumora formulations are tested to be non-comedogenic (meaning they do not clog pore channels) and do not contain animal derivatives, beeswax, or lanolin. We are strictly cruelty-free and vegan-certified.

### Q5: What should I do if a product separates or changes color slightly?
Because Lumora uses minimal artificial stabilizers, slight natural color variation in plant extracts (such as Centella Asiatica or Rosehip) between harvest seasons is normal. However, if our Pure Vitamin C serum turns dark amber or deep brown, it indicates active oxidation and should be discarded.
