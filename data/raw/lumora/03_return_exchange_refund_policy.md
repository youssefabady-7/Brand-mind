# Lumora: Official Return, Exchange, and Refund Policy

## 1. Core Return & Exchange Policy Window
At Lumora, we stand behind the integrity and gentle formulation of our skincare rituals. We accept return and exchange requests submitted within **14 calendar days** from the verified courier delivery date.

## 2. Eligibility & Public Health Hygiene Standards
Cosmetics, topical serums, and dermatological formulations are subject to strict health and safety regulations in accordance with Egyptian consumer protection standards.

To qualify for a standard return or exchange (due to change of mind or personal preference):
1. **Unopened and Factory-Sealed:** The product must remain completely unopened, unused, and with the tamper-evident metallic/plastic seal fully intact.
2. **Original Packaging:** The item must be returned in its original cardboard unit carton with all batch codes, labels, and protective caps undamaged.
3. **Proof of Purchase:** Valid order reference number (`LUM-XXXX`) or digital receipt.

## 3. Strictly Non-Returnable Items
For public hygiene, safety, and cross-contamination prevention, the following items **cannot** be returned or exchanged under any circumstances:
- Any skincare bottle, jar, dropper, or pump that has been opened, unsealed, or tested.
- Discovery kits, travel sample bundles, or complimentary promotional gifts.
- Digital or physical Lumora gift cards.

## 4. Exceptions: Defective Items and Incorrect Deliveries
We take full responsibility for packaging flaws or fulfillment discrepancies:
- **Damaged or Defective on Arrival:** If an item arrives with broken glass, a defective airless pump dispenser, or compromised packaging during courier transit, it is fully eligible for an immediate free replacement or full refund, even if unboxed. Patrons are requested to provide photographic evidence within 48 hours of delivery.
- **Incorrect Item Delivered:** If you received a product different from what you ordered, Lumora will dispatch the correct item via express courier and retrieve the incorrect unit at zero shipping expense to you.

## 5. Sensitive Case Protocol: Allergic Reactions and Irritation
Skin biology is deeply individual. In the rare event that a patron experiences an adverse reaction, allergic flare-up, or acute irritation upon testing an authorized product:
- The automated AI assistant **must not attempt automated policy resolution**.
- The inquiry is immediately escalated to our Senior Care and Dermatological Consultation Team.
- Our team will review the incident, provide soothing usage guidance, and coordinate an empathetic resolution (including specialized refunds or product substitutions).

## 6. Refund Procedures and Payout Channels
Once returned items arrive at our fulfillment hub, our quality control team inspects packaging and seal integrity within 48 hours:
- **Credit/Debit Card Payments:** Refunds are credited back to the original card within 5 to 10 banking days (subject to issuing bank processing times).
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders:** Refunds are disbursed electronically via **InstaPay**, **Vodafone Cash / Mobile Wallets**, or direct domestic Egyptian bank wire within 3 business days following successful inspection.
- Standard return courier fee of 45 EGP is deducted from change-of-mind returns, but completely waived for defective items or order fulfillment errors.
