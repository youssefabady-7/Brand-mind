# UrbanKicks: Shipping, Delivery, and Drop Fulfillment

## 1. Shipping Zones and Timelines Across Egypt
- **Cairo & Giza:** 1 to 2 business days. Express next-day dispatch on in-stock sneakers ordered before 2:00 PM.
- **Alexandria & Delta:** 2 to 3 business days.
- **Upper Egypt & Red Sea Coastal Cities:** 3 to 5 business days.

## 2. Shipping Rates
- Flat courier delivery fee across Cairo & Giza: 50 EGP.
- Delta & Canal governorates: 65 EGP.
- Upper Egypt & Frontier: 80 EGP.
- **Free Shipping Perk:** Orders over 1,500 EGP ship 100% free nationwide.

## 3. Order Verification & Delivery Process
- All sneaker deliveries include an SMS tracking link (Order format: `UK-XXXX`).
- Couriers allow open-box inspection in the presence of the courier to verify authenticity and size before final COD payment.
