# UrbanKicks: Return, Exchange, and Sizing Swap Policy

## 1. 14-Day Street Policy Window
Customers have **14 calendar days** from parcel delivery to request a return or size exchange.

## 2. Condition Rules for Sneakers and Streetwear
To protect brand authenticity and sneaker collector standards:
- **Footwear Condition:** Sneakers must be 100% unworn outdoors. Soles must be spotless, with zero dirt, scuffs, creasing, or odor. The original sneaker box must be intact and protected during return transit.
- **Apparel Condition:** Hoodies, tees, and cargo pants must have original hangtags attached and show no signs of wash or wear.
- **Non-Returnable Items:** Underwear, athletic socks, sneaker care kits with broken seals, and hype raffle drop items marked final sale.

## 3. Sizing Exchanges (Size Swaps)
If your kicks don't fit quite right, we offer one complimentary size exchange courier run per order, provided the requested replacement size is in stock.

## 4. Defective or Incorrect Items
If an item has a factory defect (delamination, sole separation) or an incorrect size/model was shipped, UrbanKicks replaces the pair immediately at zero shipping cost.
